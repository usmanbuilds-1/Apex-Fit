# Apex Fit — analytics evidence register
Updated 2026-11-28. Companion to TRAINING_ANALYTICS_TECHNICAL_PLAN.md and issue #49. This is a targeted literature and engineering review, not a systematic review, clinical validation, or claim that all features are shipped. References are linked by PMID/DOI or official documentation. Scientific findings and app policies are deliberately separate. Update this register in every feature PR with source, applicability, limitations, formula/contract and acceptance tests. Research-agent delegation was requested but no independent research-agent tool was available in this session; sources were checked directly.

## Review rules
- Record source date, title, link, study design/population, outcome and applicability. Prefer primary research, systematic reviews and official platform docs; competitor features are product examples only.
- Separate observed metrics, model estimates and user-configured policy. Do not imply population effects are individualized predictions.
- For future features, candidate sources must be rechecked and expanded before implementation; the register is not a claim of completed research or a full bibliography of every earlier conversation.
- Original technical plan remains the detailed acceptance contract. Keep PR-specific test evidence and version history. All claims of CI must reference actual commit and workflow; device/visual tests remain separate.

### A07 source recheck, 2026-11-28
Grgic et al. systematic review: [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7367986/), reviewed test-retest reliability of direct 1RM measurement (median ICC 0.97 across included estimates, not proof of individual precision). Nuzzo et al. [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933212/) pooled approximately 7,000 individuals; rep-to-%1RM relationships vary with exercise and individual. Neither study validates a historical setup confirmation or 1 g comparison precision; those are data-model decisions. Do not treat the heaviest recorded single as a measured maximal test. The exact-rep projection is a deterministic reporting metric, not a scientific prediction. User-confirmed setup uses local preferences keyed by set ID and SHA-256 source fingerprint; deleted/changed facts cannot produce records, although stale confirmations may remain until reset. Future work: transactional active-set provenance, cleanup and end-to-end export linkage, source drill-through and performance validation.

### A08 source recheck, 2026-11-28
LeSuer et al. evaluated seven prediction equations in 67 untrained college students on bench press, squat and deadlift; [paper](https://paulogentil.com/pdf/The%20Accuracy%20of%20Prediction%20Equations%20for%20Estimating%201-RM%20Performance%20in%20the%20Bench%20Press%2C%20Squat%20and%20Deadlift.pdf) lists Epley's coefficient 0.0333 (implemented as 1/30). Exercise-specific and individual error remain; no claim Epley is universally best. Nuzzo pooled 952 repetitions-to-failure tests, 7,289 individuals, 269 studies; bench and leg press relationships differ. Halperin's review examines accuracy of estimated repetitions to task failure; [study on 2-RIR](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33337690/) notes poorer accuracy at lighter loads. Therefore do not add default or estimated RIR to observed reps. Restrict to confirmed total free weights and 2–5 reps as a cautious app policy; do not encourage users to train to failure to unlock the feature. Attestation can be wrong; no individual confidence interval or measured strength claim. Prior-day weight averaging and seven-day cutoff are reporting choices. Per-hand/machine/bodyweight resistance cannot be assumed comparable. Explicit setup-specific series, formula, source sets, dated denominator and missing-state copy; edits/deletion trigger recomputation. Prior A07 confirmation encoding decodes with effort unknown. Active capture, robust transactional provenance and comparable volume-load still outstanding.

### A09 source recheck, 2026-11-28
Official Android documentation recommends transactional Room reads for one-to-many relations because the related tables are queried separately. Observable queries refresh when underlying tables change. ISO local dates and YearMonth handle month lengths and leap years. The actual/projection split and refusing retroactive schedule classification are product data-integrity policies, not exercise-science claims. Initial calendar reads saved sessions and one valid active plan snapshot; no historical schedule ledger yet. A future plan may change and a missing historical log does not prove rest or a missed workout. Existing rotation semantics are reused, not reimplemented. Tests exercise multiple sessions, partial-only days, invalid schedules, no retroactive changes, leap year and cycle rollover.

### A10 source recheck, 2026-11-28
ACSM 2026 distinguishes weekly set counts from volume load (load × reps × sets) and notes the latter was rarely considered in included reviews. Baz-Valle et al. support set counts as an alternative volume metric for hypertrophy in appropriate contexts; a 2022 volume-matched meta-analysis found higher loads produced greater maximal strength even at matched volume load. Therefore neither kg-reps nor sets are interchangeable with physiological stimulus. Record load groups by exercise, user-confirmed setup and basis; do not add per-hand, stack and total external mass into one purported tonnage. Report duration is the saved duration, not active lifting time; exact-rep events are reconstructed from current saved history rather than original immutable receipts. Room transaction reads session and related sets atomically; record-history/confirmation streams are separate and may briefly differ during edits. CSV source IDs/occurrence/timestamps and unrestricted workout-set history added; nutrition and older PR export limits remain and export is not a transactional full backup. Pending: persistent original receipt/attribution, atomic historical schedule provenance, end-to-end export and device/render QA.

### A11 source recheck, 2026-11-28
The 2024 review covers insufficiently active adults and reports positive physical-activity effects, with limited evidence across goal types and psychological outcomes. The 2026 Exercise and Sports Science Australia expert statement emphasizes tailored goal setting, feedback and self-monitoring; it is practice guidance, not proof that this implementation improves strength or body composition. Use measurable user-chosen thresholds and evidence-linked progress, with optional dates rather than inferred achievement dates. Goal starts tomorrow to avoid same-day historical ambiguity; prior strength baseline is the best qualifying observation, bodyweight baseline is the latest prior daily average within seven days; cumulative tracking starts at zero by definition. These are reporting policies. Goal success requires load and rep criteria in the same saved set; volume is one saved session with matching confirmed setup and load basis, not a sum of unlike exercises or sessions. Bodyweight thresholds are observed daily means, not sustained or medically appropriate weight change; no deficit or calorie target prescribed. Goal definitions are stored locally in DataStore, immutable except archival status; deletion removes only definition, and source corrections can remove an achievement. CSV export includes definitions, but end-to-end verification and direct exercise-detail entry/edit-as-replacement UX remain outstanding. Inputs currently use explicit kg units, independent of display preference. No claim of full F11 acceptance before CI, rendered/device QA and provenance review.

### A12 source recheck, 2026-11-28
Android Photo Picker grants access to selected media without broad library permissions; the default grant is temporary. Store independently decoded copies in noBackupFilesDir, which official Auto Backup documentation identifies as always excluded; allowBackup=false is already present. Device-to-device and OEM transfer behavior must still be checked on real devices before release. No FileProvider path for internal photos, no network or CSV photo path. File metadata lives in a UUID/date/pose directory name; image and thumbnail are staged together and published via a directory rename. Source bytes are temporary in the same private directory and deleted before commit; a crashed import is cleaned on next store access. Fresh JPEG encoding removes source EXIF/GPS/XMP rather than copying original file metadata; Android decoder/Exif orientation behavior needs real format/device tests. 32 MB input, 2048px maximum sampled dimension and 480px thumbnail are product resource bounds, not scientific thresholds. Delete unlinks private copies and leaves original gallery photo intact; does not guarantee forensic erasure on flash storage. No body-fat or physique prediction: lighting, pose, distance and clothing confound visual comparison. Pending: actual device backup/transfer validation, EXIF and orientation fixtures across formats, process death/import/reset races, memory testing, screenshot and device review. No claim of medical accuracy or hardware-backed encryption.

### A13 source recheck, 2026-11-28
The 2025 review (DOI 10.1007/s40279-025-02176-8) found similar pooled chronic strength/endurance/hypertrophy and total repetitions/volume load compared with traditional prescriptions, shorter sessions and higher perceived exertion. The 2026 ACSM overview reports set structure does not consistently influence training outcomes. Thus superset/circuit grouping is optional, not extra stimulus or a mandate to shorten rest. Configurable 1–3600s rest bounds reflect existing checkpoint invariants; 15/90s editor defaults are editable product defaults, not an evidence-based prescription. Current slice groups only the first consecutive exercise occurrences of a single active workout, before logging. No plan or importer grouping is silently inferred. Cursor and group persist in active JSON v3; v2 keeps its stable session ID and legacy v1 retains conservative recovery. Uneven counts bypass exhausted/skipped members; configured intervals remain distinct from observed rest. Group set counts are locked while active, and ordinary sequential behavior is preserved. Session note retains configuration but historical per-set group/round fields are not yet stored. Future work: multiple/arbitrary plan groups, explicit importer confirmation, zero-rest transitions, add/undo/correction policies and UI, source group history, integration/device recovery tests and actual screen approval. No claim that F13 is fully accepted.

### A14 source recheck, 2026-11-28
The 2023 drop-set systematic review found similar hypertrophy with time-efficiency benefits; the 2026 advanced-methods review reports a modest rest-pause signal but no meaningful overall hypertrophy superiority when volume/intensity/effort are comparable. The 2026 drop-set meta-analysis highlights protocol heterogeneity. None supports universal fractional or extra set credit for each continuation. Store a parent working bout with actual initial reps/load and a versioned continuation payload (canonical kg, reps, preceding rest seconds, explicitly selected failure/unknown/not-failure). Drop loads must decline; rest-pause loads must match and rest must be positive; these are operational classification rules, not optimal rest prescriptions. No fabricated RPE or failure. Parent counts once in set-count analytics; report sums actual segment reps, observed strength shows only the first uninterrupted pair. Advanced setups are not promoted into confirmed PR/e1RM or comparable volume totals; existing legacy PR updates and stalled-count updates are suppressed for exercises with advanced records in that session, and latest advanced history pauses progression at next session start. This is deliberately conservative. Other legacy totals/receipt metrics may still reflect only parent load/reps; reconcile them before acceptance. Current UI logs the full performed bout atomically in a single editor; not yet live segment-by-segment logging, grouped circuits, plan templates, editable advanced sets, or new warm-up UI. Existing warm-up field stays distinct. Nullable migration 29→30 preserves legacy rows; active v4 preserves v2/v3 stable ID. Test migration and full chain, crash recovery, report count, invalid values, codec and interaction behavior. CI/device/screenshot approval and full metric parity remain required. No automatic training-to-failure advice.

### Integration: historical attribution snapshot (2026-11-28)
This is a data integrity change, not a new physiology claim. Direct muscle is the original logged label; secondary catalog labels are captured at active occurrence creation and frozen on each saved set. They remain catalog classifications, not muscle activation or hypertrophy measurements. Legacy rows are not backfilled, and explicitly empty snapshots differ from unknown. Existing indirect set proxy remains a reporting convention; captured and inferred sources still require explicit coverage UI. See official Room migration guidance (https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room/migrating-db-versions) for additive migration and generated schema testing. No change to RIR, e1RM, automatic progression or scientific thresholds in this slice.

### Integration: explicit RIR selection (2026-11-28)
RIR is a self-reported effort estimate, not a measurement; prior RIR accuracy evidence in A08 supports distinguishing ratings from missing information. The completion UI no longer selects 2 RIR automatically; unselected effort uses RPE 0 and no actual RIR value is supplied to the save call. The existing 5+ bucket maps to the app's coarse RPE convention, not a calibrated conversion. Legacy non-null RIR storage still requires a separate nullable observation/source field. No new biological claim or training-to-failure recommendation.

### Integration: captured set observations (2026-11-28)
A08 eligibility and uncertainty policies are unchanged. Actual load convention and RIR are user attestations, not measured resistance or objectively verified proximity to failure. Explicit 5+ is a category; legacy values are not promoted. Setup names identify user-selected comparable conditions, not calibrated machines. Optional explicit free-weight rep-limit check retains narrow estimate eligibility. This is provenance engineering; no new physiological effect or accuracy claim. See A07/A08 evidence and Android Room migration guidance. Existing automated progression consumers still need source-aware review; no new prescriptions.

### Integration: progression eligibility (2026-11-28)
Explicit matching setup and rated work are reporting/decision integrity policies, not physiological measurements. RPE 8 and existing metadata increments are product rules; they are not validated as universal optimal prescriptions. Unknown effort and mixed resistance conventions cannot trigger increases or stall classifications. Until matched historical prescription replay exists, hold or offer a conditional next-load suggestion rather than automatically deloading. Existing A08 RIR evidence applies; no new empirical claim.

### A09/A12 — schedule completion and missing outcomes
The review of exercise-class adherence measures ([2016, PMID 27338884](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27338884/), [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4932302/)) distinguishes attendance, duration, intensity and retention/completion; the evidence is in older-adult exercise classes, not validation of a resistance-training app metric. CONSORT 2025 explanation ([BMJ 389:e081124](https://www.bmj.com/content/389/bmj-2024-081124)) emphasizes reporting missing outcomes, assumptions and sensitivity analyses in trials; applied here only as a reporting principle. Define C full completions, P partial, M explicit missed, E excused, U unconfirmed. Eligible N=C+P+M+U; E displayed separately. Full completion bounds C/N to (C+U)/N; point only if U=0, N>0. Outcome coverage=(C+P+M)/N, schedule coverage=recorded training/rest dates divided by elapsed calendar dates. These are exact descriptive arithmetic on recorded schedule, not statistical confidence limits, clinical adherence or a calibrated dose/health measure. No arbitrary fractional credit, score cutoff or unknown-as-failure assumption. Calendar today/future not evaluated; unknown schedule dates outside denominator explicitly counted. Product definition, not universal exercise adherence standard.

### A12 period training report
Uses existing A04 matched-date reporting policy and A10 saved-workout metric definitions above, not a new biological model. Finished local days only (today excluded for all periods), calendar Monday start and real calendar month lengths, matched first N days of current and immediately preceding period (N = min(elapsed current days, preceding period days)). Actual logs are observational; unknown exercise outside app is not zero. Completed working bouts are counts of persisted parents, advanced continuations add reps but not extra independent sets. Duration is recorded workout duration, not active lifting time; disclose coverage and avoid duration comparison with missing records. Schedule completion follows A09/A12 evidence coverage and deterministic bounds. No claims of improved hypertrophy, strength or adherence outside recorded schedule.

### Reservation-aware daily targets and reminders
ISSN nutrient timing position stand (Kerksick et al., 2017, DOI 10.1186/s12970-017-0189-4, PMID 28919842) prioritizes sufficient daily protein and practical intake patterns. ISSN protein and exercise (Jäger et al., 2017, DOI 10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8, PMID 28642676) supports daily protein sufficiency. Neither validates fixed 100–200 kcal cycling bonuses or a specific daily energy adjustment from a moved workout. Existing cycling is product policy, not measured energy expenditure; moving one reservation does not alter adaptive TDEE. Recurring frequency remains baseline, exact cross-week planned budget accounting not yet implemented. Notification suppression/date validation are engineering correctness policies, not claims about behavior or health outcomes.

### Weekly calorie allocation
Exact sum of planned calories is an arithmetic invariant for a fixed projection, not a biological prediction or evidence that cycling improves hypertrophy. Retains existing protein/fat targets and safety guardrails. Distributes integer rounding remainder across rest dates; grams may have normal rounding discrepancy. No retroactive debt or compensatory restriction for missed training. Prior displayed targets are not recoverable from a mutable projection. Long-run adaptive TDEE still uses recurring frequency/observed weight and intake, not one-off moves.

### Permanent supersets and circuits
2025 systematic review/meta-analysis, PMID 39903375, DOI 10.1007/s40279-025-02176-8: pooled estimates suggest supersets can reduce session duration and increase time efficiency, with broadly similar chronic strength/hypertrophy outcomes and higher perceived exertion; heterogeneous evidence, not proof of universal equivalence or superiority. 2024 whole-body multi-joint RCT PMID 39072654 found a strength advantage for traditional sets in pull-down, reinforcing that grouping should be user-selected rather than automatic. Store explicit rest between members and rounds; do not impose a fixed optimal rest or infer physiological benefit. Preserve occurrence-level actual set counts and observed rest, no extra hypertrophy credit from grouping.

### Group context in progression
Existing superset evidence supports time efficiency but higher exertion under some protocols; it does not establish interchangeable RIR/rep-load progression across grouped versus sequential training. Excluding grouped/unknown sessions from sequential progression is a conservative product policy, not a quantified biological correction. Count actual sets/reps unchanged; no additional effective-set credit or automatic fatigue adjustment for group membership. New structured session format permits later context-specific comparisons; missing legacy context remains unknown.

### Comparable performance across training formats
Superset evidence cited above supports treating format as context, not a fixed load correction. Stratification by recorded session format is a product comparability safeguard, not a validated adjustment. Even within grouped/unknown buckets, rest and order may differ. Epley estimates remain conditional on explicitly confirmed low-rep free-weight rep-limit sets; grouped estimates are not fresh maximal tests. Raw set totals and actual recorded achievements remain intact.
