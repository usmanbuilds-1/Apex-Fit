IMPORTANT IMAGE-RIGHTS NOTE
The upstream project owner has stated that the origin/royalty status of the bundled exercise images is unknown and that image use is at the user's own risk:
https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/issues/2

Apex Fit therefore does not construct, fetch, display, bundle, or claim public-domain rights to those exercise image files in production.

app/src/main/assets/exercise_media.json may retain upstream source IDs, image-path metadata, and written instructions for mapping/provenance. Written instructions remain usable independently of the image paths.

YouTube technique mappings are stored in exercise_videos.json and streamed through YouTube's embedded player. Apex Fit does not download or rehost the audiovisual content.
